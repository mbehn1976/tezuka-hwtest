## Peak markers on all four spectrum pages, firmware images tested on both boards (commits b1e2a6c, 34b37a9)

**Change.** The two peak markers (`P1`/`P2`, frequency in MHz and level in dB) are now also on the Radio Astronomy page (header field `PEAK ON/OFF`) and on the Signal Classifier plot (always on), using the same code as the Spectrum page. They were already on the Spectrum and Complex I/Q pages.

**Images.** Pluto flashed from the .frm, Pluto+ from the SD image. Both serve the page from the image (the /root/index.html md5 equals the build output) and the bitstreams match their packages.

**RF suite** (I/Q check with automatic carrier offset, phase check and frame phase check at 2.5, 10, 20, 30.72 and 61.44 MS/s on each board): 30 steps, 70 checks ok, 0 FAIL, every settings restore matched.

* Frame rotation measured minus predicted: -0.0010 to +0.0022 rad, scatter at most 0.0019 rad, no frame off by more than 0.3 rad.
* OFDM log correlation 0.987 to 0.999; phase derived frequency minus time domain frequency -12.1 to +3.2 Hz; model error at most 2.04 deg and 0.48 dB.
* The Pluto+ 2.5 MS/s check that failed once in the previous run (single-bin criterion, see the comment on PR 12) passed.

**Markers on the pages served by the boards** (unequal two tones at 10 MS/s, the other board transmits):

| Page, served by | P1 | P2 |
|---|---|---|
| Classifier, Pluto | 2400.869565 MHz, 93.6 dB | 2400.891549 MHz, 81.1 dB |
| Radio Astronomy, Pluto | 2400.870176 MHz, 102.3 dB | 2400.890327 MHz, 90.2 dB |
| Classifier, Pluto+ | 2400.771861 MHz, 92.7 dB | 2400.791402 MHz, 81.2 dB |
| Radio Astronomy, Pluto+ | 2400.770640 MHz, 102.2 dB | 2400.192965 MHz, 92.0 dB |

On three of the four views P2 is the second tone and the level difference is 12.1 to 12.5 dB, as sent. The last row is the exception: P2 is a spur at 2400.193 MHz, stronger than the second tone. It appears when the Pluto transmits and the Pluto+ receives and was absent when the Pluto received, so it is probably a spur of the transmitting Pluto seen through the 2.5 MS/s view. I have not verified that. The marker reports the second highest bin of what the page displays. I first described this spur as a leftover of the page's averaging window; it came back on a fresh page, so that was wrong, and the README is corrected.
