## Board-served tests: Complex I/Q views, Radio Astronomy CAL and FSW (commit 0087bfe)

Firmware images newdash4, pages served by the boards themselves. Unequal two tones (the second tone 12.4 dB below the first), one board transmits, the other receives.

**Real/Imaginary, Bode and Smith views.** I decoded the same I/Q stream independently in Python, then read the page.

| Board | Python decode | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Pluto+ (2.5 MS/s) | bin 3311, 2400.770874 MHz, 65.1 dB; bin 3344, 2400.791016 MHz, 52.7 dB | same bins and frequencies, 65.0/65.1 dB and 52.7 dB |
| Pluto (10 MS/s) | bin 2404, 2400.869141 MHz, 66.6 dB; bin 2412, 2400.888672 MHz, 54.4 dB | same bins and frequencies, 66.5/66.6 dB and 54.5 dB |

* Real/Imaginary (unit circle): the peak is 1.000 + j0.000 on both boards. The second tone reads 0.164 + j0.176 (Pluto+) and 0.193 + j0.156 (Pluto). Recomputed from the page's own magnitude and phase readout: 0.164 + j0.175 and 0.193 + j0.156.
* Bode: the offset of the peak from the LO reads 770.9 kHz (Pluto+, bin 3311) and 869.1 kHz (Pluto, bin 2404), as expected for those bins.
* Smith chart: the peak at 1.00 + j0.00, the second tone at 0.18 + j0.16 (Pluto+) and -0.14 - j0.21 (Pluto), |Gamma| 0.24 and 0.25. Recomputed from the readout phases: 0.184 + j0.155 and -0.137 - j0.207.

**Radio Astronomy CAL.** Transmitter silenced (TX gain -89 dB), CAL pressed with 12 to 15 frames in the window (ratio about 0 dB, markers at 0.1 dB), then the tones switched on and AUTO pressed. Pluto+ page: P1 2400.771251 MHz +78.3 dB, P2 2400.792013 MHz +66.2 dB (12.1 dB apart, as sent). Pluto page: P1 about +66 dB, P2 about +55 dB. The markers follow the largest ratio bins and the auto scale fits them.

**Radio Astronomy FSW** (Pluto+ page, INTEG 5 s, OFS 500 kHz). The receive LO read over ssh alternates between 2400.000 and 2400.500 MHz, and the receiver sees the tone at two different bins (103 dB over a 29 dB median). The page, however, shows SIG - REF only between about -16 and +8 dB, where two clean dwells of a strong CW tone would give about +/-70 dB. My explanation, from reading the code and not measured: the retune is an MQTT command, so the first frames of each dwell are still at the old LO, and a leak of a few percent of a tone that is 70 dB above the noise caps the ratio near 10 dB. FSW is meant for weak lines, so a strong CW tone is not a good test of its ratio; I would not call this a bug without a weak-line test. With INTEG 1 s the trace is unusable (one frame per dwell).

Not tested: other boards, FSW with a weak line, CAL/FSW together with a changing LO other than the page's own.
