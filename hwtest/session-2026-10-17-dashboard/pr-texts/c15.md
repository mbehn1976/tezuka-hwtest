## rf_phase_check: frame alignment near a half-bin tone (commit 5006f53), full suite result

While running the suite on a firmware image I got one printed (not scored) outlier from `rf_phase_check.py`: Pluto, 2.5 MS/s, tone 205 kHz, model phase error 178.9 deg, amplitude error 88 dB, I/Q against raw 178.9 deg, with the tone 0.51 of a bin from a bin centre. All earlier runs of that item were at most 0.5 deg and 0.22 dB.

**Cause (reproduced on synthetic captures, not on the boards).** The script aligned the 63 raw frames on the strongest raw position and assumed that was the bin nearest the expected frequency. Near a half-bin position two bins are about equally strong, so about half of the captures were aligned one bin position off and every row mixed two frames. In addition the bins at +-3 are about 54 dB below the peak at that position, close to the noise, so the ratio of data to model there is meaningless. The model error numbers are printed but not part of the pass criteria, which is why the run still passed.

**Fix.** The alignment tries the neighbouring bins and keeps the one with strong bins all around it. The model comparison fits a complex amplitude per frame instead of dividing by one reference bin. Only the bins -3..3 that are at least 20 dB above the noise floor are scored (`model_bins` and `bin_fraction` are printed). Pass criteria unchanged.

**Evidence.**

* Synthetic captures, tone at fractional bin positions 0 to 1, SNR 62, 50 and 40 dB: old analysis up to 180 deg and 45 dB near the half-bin position, new one at most 1.3 deg and 0.23 dB at every position and SNR.
* Boards, received tone swept through the half-bin region (20 captures, old and new analysis on the same data): at most 0.4 deg and 2.1 deg (I/Q against raw) near half-bin, old up to 2.3 deg and 38 deg. The 178.9 deg outlier itself did not recur on the boards.
* Full suite with the fixed script (I/Q check, phase check and frame phase check at 2.5, 10, 20, 30.72 and 61.44 MS/s on both boards, firmware image with the new dashboard): 30 steps, 70 checks ok, 0 FAIL, every settings restore matched. Phase check model error 0.07 to 0.92 deg and at most 0.25 dB, I/Q against raw at most 5.1 deg, 6 or 7 bins scored per tone, the fractional bin positions of the 30 tones spread over 0.0 to 0.99 (0.47, 0.47, 0.52 and 0.58 among them). Frame rotation measured minus predicted -0.0009 to +0.0022 rad, scatter at most 0.0020 rad; OFDM log correlation 0.986 to 0.999.

That is one suite run, so it does not prove the old outlier cannot recur; it shows the fixed script handles half-bin tones on both boards at all five rates. `pycodestyle --exclude maia-hdl/adi-hdl/ maia-hdl/` is clean on the branch.
