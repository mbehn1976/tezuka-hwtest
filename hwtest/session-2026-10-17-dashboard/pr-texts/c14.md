## Correction: cause of the 178.9 deg model error in rf_phase_check (maia-sdr PR 12, commit 5006f53)

In my previous comment (https://github.com/F5OEO/tezuka_fw/pull/500#issuecomment-6237269891) I attributed the one outlier of the newdash5 suite (Pluto, 2.5 MS/s, tone 205 kHz: model phase error 178.9 deg, amplitude error 88 dB, I/Q against raw 178.9 deg) to the model fit picking a different peak bin at a half-bin position. That was only partly right. It is a bug in the analysis script, not in the firmware, and I have reproduced and fixed it.

**Cause.** `rf_phase_check.py` aligned the 63 raw frames on the strongest raw position, assuming it was the bin nearest the expected frequency. With the tone near a half-bin position two bins are about equally strong, so about half of the captures were aligned one bin position off, and every row of the frame matrix then mixed two frames. In addition, at that position the bins at +-3 are about 54 dB below the peak, close to the noise, and the ratio of data to model there is meaningless. The model errors are printed but not part of the pass criteria, which is why the run still counted as ok.

**Evidence.** On synthetic captures (tone at fractional bin positions 0 to 1, SNR 62, 50 and 40 dB) the old analysis gave up to 180 deg and 45 dB near the half-bin position; the fixed one stays within 1.3 deg and 0.23 dB at every position and SNR. On the boards, with the received tone swept through the half-bin region (20 captures, old and new analysis on the same data): old up to 2.3 deg and 38 deg for I/Q against raw, new at most 0.4 deg and 2.1 deg there. The 178.9 deg outlier itself did not recur on the boards, so I cannot show the old code failing on this rig; the reproduction is in simulation. The fixed script ran at 2.5 and 61.44 MS/s on both boards: all tones ok, model error at most 0.9 deg and 0.19 dB, every restore matched.

**Fix** (maia-sdr PR 12, commit 5006f53): the alignment tries the neighbouring bins and keeps the one with strong bins all around it, the model comparison fits a complex amplitude per frame instead of dividing by one reference bin, and only the bins -3..3 that are at least 20 dB above the noise floor are scored (`model_bins` and `bin_fraction` are printed). The pass criteria are unchanged. It is the same family as the single-bin criterion relaxed in `rf_iq_check.py` (a tone near a half-bin position), but a different mechanism. I have not re-run the full suite with the new script, only the checks above.
