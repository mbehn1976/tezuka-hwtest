## Radio Astronomy FSW drops the stale frames, firmware images tested on both boards (commits ceaafde, 18d302e)

**Change.** Following the measurement in my previous comment (https://github.com/F5OEO/tezuka_fw/pull/500#issuecomment-6236233337), the Radio Astronomy page now drops the frame that triggers each SIG/REF flip (it was taken before the retune) and the next one (still integrating while the LO moves). The new dwell starts with the third frame, so a dwell holds INTEG - 2 clean frames. It is a short counter in `radioastro.jsx` (`fswSkipRef`). The ratio of a strong line is no longer limited by a stale fraction of the window.

**Images.** Pluto flashed from the .frm, Pluto+ from the SD image. Both serve a page identical to a fresh bundle of the merged source, and the bitstreams match their packages.

**RF suite** (I/Q check with automatic offset, phase check, frame phase check at 2.5, 10, 20, 30.72 and 61.44 MS/s on each board): 30 steps, 70 checks ok, 0 FAIL, every settings restore matched.

* Frame rotation measured minus predicted: -0.0008 to +0.0022 rad, scatter at most 0.0020 rad, no frame off by more than 0.3 rad.
* OFDM log correlation 0.988 to 0.999; phase derived frequency minus time domain frequency -11.6 to +3.7 Hz.
* One outlier in numbers that are printed but not scored: Pluto phase check at 2.5 MS/s, tone 205 kHz, model phase error 178.9 deg, amplitude error 88.5 dB, I/Q against raw 178.9 deg (all earlier runs of that item: at most 0.5 deg, 0.22 dB, 14 deg). The tone sat 0.51 of a bin from a bin centre. Four re-runs (12 tones) gave at most 1.8 deg and 0.37 dB; the one other tone at a fractional bin position of 0.52 gave 64.7 deg for I/Q against raw. My reading, not proven: at a half-bin position two bins are about equally strong and the peak bin picked by the model fit can differ from the one used for the measurement, so the ratios to the neighbours differ by the window's sidelobe sign flips. It is a weakness of the analysis there, not a data error; the pass criteria (frequency, scatter, peak bin) were met.
